About the Treatment

Full Body Relaxation Massage

At MiST, our Full Body Relaxation Massage is performed by a trained therapist using considered, comforting technique, adapted to your comfort and preference. This service is offered as a relaxation treatment and is not intended to treat any medical condition. Exact massage offerings are being finalised, please confirm current availability with our team when booking.
