Welcome to MiST

Your Beauty, Your Signature.

MiST was founded on a simple idea, that a salon visit should feel considered, not rushed. Every service, from a first haircut to a full bridal transformation, begins with a conversation about you: your features, your lifestyle and the look you're hoping to leave with.

We bring together hair, skin, makeup, permanent makeup, nails and modern aesthetic care under one roof, in a space designed to feel calm, elegant and entirely unisex, beauty, on your terms.
