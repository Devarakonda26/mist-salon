About MiST

A premium unisex salon, PMU and aesthetics studio in the heart of Kurnool.

Our Story

Beauty, built around you.

MiST began with a straightforward belief: that a great salon experience shouldn't feel like a production line. It should feel personal, a space where your stylist actually listens, where your therapist explains what she's doing and why, and where the result reflects you rather than a trend.

Today, MiST brings hair, skin, makeup, permanent makeup, nails and modern aesthetic services together under one roof in Kurnool, built for every kind of client, for him, for her, for every kind of beautiful.

Our Philosophy

Considered, never rushed.

Every appointment at MiST starts with a conversation, not a script. We believe the best results come from understanding your hair, skin and features first, then applying technique, not the other way around.

We hold ourselves to a standard of hygiene, professionalism and attentiveness that we'd want for our own visits, because that's ultimately what a premium salon experience should feel like.

Our Approach

A professional team, a premium environment.

Our stylists, therapists and technicians bring focused expertise across hair, skin, makeup, PMU, nails and aesthetics, trained to explain, not just perform, every step of your service.

The studio itself is designed to match that standard: considered lighting, clean lines and a genuinely unisex environment where every client feels equally at home.
