Everyday Hair Care Tips Between Salon Visits

April 18, 2026

Reducing heat styling frequency, or using a heat protectant when you do style, is one of the simplest ways to keep hair looking healthier for longer between salon visits.

A weekly hair spa or deep conditioning treatment at home can help maintain the softness and manageability you feel right after a salon treatment.

And if you're colour-treated, sulphate-free, colour-safe shampoo helps protect your investment and keep tones looking fresher for longer.
